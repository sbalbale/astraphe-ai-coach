# ASTRAPHE AI Coach — Release Notes (v2.0.0)

**Tag:** `v2.0.0`
**Base:** Builds on [v1.0.0](https://github.com/sbalbale/astraphe-ai-coach/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

## Overview

v2.0.0 rolls up three months of work since the open source launch. The app has a new look (the Graphite + Sky theme, a new logo, and real tablet and desktop layouts), and the coach can now be reached outside the app: by iMessage, through push notifications, and from any MCP-compatible AI client. Plans can be imported in one step, edited by hand, and compared against what was actually recorded. The Body tab gains all-day heart rate, automatic workout detection, weight tracking, and a sleep need model, and day strain now counts the whole day rather than just workouts. Underneath, a security audit closed a set of critical and high findings, and dozens of data-correctness fixes make every number agree across screens.

## New Ways to Reach Your Data and Coach

### Astraphe MCP Server (#26, #27)
- A standalone, **read-only** MCP server lets you connect your Astraphe data to Claude Desktop, claude.ai Connectors, Claude Code, or any other MCP client.
- Its 12 tools wrap the same tool handlers the in-app coach already uses, so behavior and test coverage are shared.
- Authorization uses Supabase Auth's OAuth 2.1 Server mode, so tokens are ordinary Supabase JWTs and existing row-level security scopes every call. A consent screen lives at `/oauth/consent`.
- Rate limited, with its own CI test and build jobs. See [`docs/MCP_SERVER.md`](https://github.com/sbalbale/astraphe-ai-coach/blob/main/docs/MCP_SERVER.md).

### Text the Coach over iMessage (#33, #37)
- Text the coach from your phone and get a text after each workout. Texts share the app's coach chat, so a thread started by text shows up in the app and the other way round.
- A small Node bridge (`photon-bridge/`) holds the Photon stream; `/new` starts a fresh conversation.
- Post-workout texts skip backfills and short or old workouts, and are claimed atomically so two replicas never double-text. See [`docs/PHOTON_MESSAGING.md`](https://github.com/sbalbale/astraphe-ai-coach/blob/main/docs/PHOTON_MESSAGING.md).

### Coach Notifications (#49, #64, #86, #87)
- **Post-workout debrief:** a coach thread with a context card and quick replies, sent right away or in a 19:00 evening digest.
- **Coach replies** arrive as a push once you've left the chat, and never while the chat is open on any device.
- **Morning readiness**, a **weekly summary** (Sun–Sat), and **sync-problem** alerts when a connected app goes quiet for two days.
- Quiet hours (default 22:00–07:00), no double sends across replicas or restarts, and notifications that open their own thread or page.

## A New Look

### Graphite + Sky Theme and Navigation (#40, #42)

### Tablet and Desktop Layouts (#45)
- Wide screens (≥768 px) get a sidebar or icon rail, a **⌘K / Ctrl+K command palette**, and `1`–`5` section shortcuts.
- Six wide screens: Today, Training log (master-detail), Trends & zones, Body, Plan (month/week grid with an inspector, drag-and-drop, and Undo), and a three-pane Coach.

## Training Plans

- **Import a whole plan in one step** (#46, #52, #53): the coach's `import_plan` tool handles long plans and runs on strong models only. Weeks now run Sunday–Saturday.
- **Edit or plan a session by hand** (#55), on phone and wide screens. A session's length and planned TSS come from its blocks and follow coach edits (#57, #75, #85).
- **Planned vs actual** (#56, #67, #72, #77): recorded workouts link to planned sessions, with target windows and a block list. Power is judged against its target on a 30-second rolling average.
- Today shows every session for the day, with readable notes instead of raw tables or JSON (#54).
- Workout blocks display in minutes and seconds, and time fields accept `h:mm:ss`, treat `.` as `:`, and fill in from the right like a timer (#50, #58, #59, #60).

## Body: Heart Rate, Weight, Sleep, and Strain

- **All-day heart rate** (#89): Garmin's all-day series is now stored and shown on a new Heart rate screen, with sleep and workout bands, drag-to-zoom, time in zone, and resting HR against its 30-day average.
- **Workout auto-detection** (#89): sessions of 10+ minutes at 40%+ of heart-rate reserve are saved as detected workouts. A recorded workout always wins, and a dismissed detection stays dismissed.
- **Weight** (#89): a new Weight screen and weigh-in history with optional body fat and undoable deletes; profile edits log a real weigh-in.
- **Measured resting HR** (#89), and an estimated max HR when an athlete hasn't set one (#74).
- **Day strain counts the whole day** (#90): all-day heart rate above 50% of max HR now adds to strain outside workouts, Garmin's carried-forward flatlines are ignored, and the strain breakdown shows the all-day part. Charts are touch-friendly.
- **Sleep need** (#85): naps no longer inflate the night, sleep debt carries over with caps, and a new card shows last night's sleep against need and tonight's target.
- Garmin sleep now includes **SpO2 and skin temperature deviation** (#31).
- The previous night's sleep and recovery are no longer shown as today's before they sync (#84).

## AI Coach

- **Photos and documents** reach the model (#46).
- **Self-hosted model option** (#66, #83): the coach chat can run on a self-hosted OpenAI-compatible model while Gemini handles everything else. It is off by default and controlled by `CHAT_LLM_PROVIDER` / `CHAT_MODEL`; Gemini takes over when a turn fails, within a 240-second turn limit.
- **Trustworthy edits** (#47, #48, #51): the coach reads the calendar before editing, replaces like for like, checks weekdays, never wipes the plan from chat, and never claims a change that wasn't saved.
- **Better grounding** (#62, #85, #88): it uses the profile FTP rather than a stale memory, stops suggesting past races as goals, writes durations as words, and gains an `update_profile` tool.
- **Web search** (#35, #36, #88): SearXNG-backed `web_search` that works on Gemini 3 models, with tool-call signatures preserved between hops.
- **Faster** chat and post-workout texts (#39), with no leaked reasoning (#88) and correct rendering of replies containing HTML tags or tables (#65, #70).
- Empty "New chat" conversations stay out of the history (#61), and timeouts fail over cleanly (#44).

## Data Correctness

- **TSS** scores power with the athlete's FTP and pace in their own units (#71).
- **Strain** reflects each workout's real contribution, and a re-sync without zones no longer resets a workout's strain to 0 (#68, #69).
- **Multisport** legs, rowing, TSS-0 workouts, strength strain, unstaged sleep, and reprocess order are all handled correctly (#73).
- **Garmin**: a near-zero speed isn't treated as a pace, and one bad activity no longer aborts a sync (#78).
- **Long histories** are read newest first and paged, which restores the sleep timeline (#82), and older workouts and biometrics load when a view reaches back (#79, #63).
- **One definition per number** across every screen (#80, #81).

## Security

From the November 2026 codebase and security audit (#41, #43):

- Fixed a coach-reply XSS.
- OAuth state is signed and single-use, so a provider account can't be linked to someone else's athlete, and post-OAuth redirects are strict.
- Users can no longer write `oauth_tokens`, and third-party OAuth/MCP tokens are rejected at the API and read-only at the database.
- Clickjacking headers, an authenticated Garmin webhook, a WHOOP webhook replay check, a Strava subscription ID check, and right-most-hop `X-Forwarded-For` handling for rate limits.

## Upgrade Notes

1. **Run the new database migrations** (weigh-ins, all-day heart rate, notification log, detection dismissals, and others). The reference deploy applies them automatically.
2. **Optional components:** the MCP server needs Supabase Auth's OAuth 2.1 Server mode enabled; the Photon bridge needs `PHOTON_*` settings. Both are separate deployments and can be left out.
3. **Self-hosted coach model** is opt-in: set `CHAT_LLM_PROVIDER=openai`, `CHAT_MODEL`, `OPENAI_API_BASE_URL`, and `OPENAI_API_KEY` to use it.
